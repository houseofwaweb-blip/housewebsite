# Copy decision — current site vs ChatGPT revision, judged against the build spec

Two reviewers went section by section through the current site copy and ChatGPT's revision, scored against the build spec. Their full per-section verdicts are in Parts 1 and 2 below. This top section is the chair's synthesis: the pattern, the blocking fixes, and the business facts only you can settle.

## The pattern (both reviewers agreed)
- **Take ChatGPT's wording wherever the current copy states an unverified fact.** Review scores, credentials, stats, delivery, donations, press hits, client names — the current copy asserts these as fact; ChatGPT's honest, conditional versions are the ones that pass the spec (§7.4, §11, §12, §27, §31).
- **Keep the current copy wherever the strength is voice or ceremony, not a claim.** The revision over-hedges and drains the House voice for no honesty gain — so current wins on the Artwork page, Philosophy, the Store section intros, the "Role of HoWA" copy (near-verbatim the spec's own language), the requestable service detail pages, how-it-works, My House, and the just-corrected service descriptions.
- **One hybrid:** Gardens' "Assistant" framing → take ChatGPT's "Powered by HoWA" wording (current breaks the §2.2 naming rule).

## BLOCKING — fabricated claims to fix before anything ships
These are not style calls; they assert things as fact that aren't verified, and the spec forbids them.
1. **Placeholder review scores** ("4.9 / 342 verified visits" on service pages) and the homepage **"5.0 verified rating"** — remove/hide until a real ratings source exists. (§7.4, §27)
2. **Credentials** — the universal badge set (Guild of Master Craftsmen, Carbon Neutral, Safe Contractor) applied to every service, and blanket lines like "every professional is background-checked, insured and reviewed" — use real, per-service badges only. (§7.4)
3. **Hard insurance stats** (70% / 66% / 40%) and **Benefact donation figures** — take ChatGPT's conditional wording or confirm the exact figures with compliance. (§11, §27, §31)
4. **Named press hits and client projects** (incl. Delve Interiors) — do not publish until consent/verification. (§31)
5. **Hardcoded "free UK delivery"** on the PDP — take the conditional version (delivery is Shopify data). (§12)
6. **All six Offers** publish placeholder prices/dates/benefits — take ChatGPT's conditional wording until each offer is confirmed.

**Safe to action immediately** (removing a fabrication is always safe): items 1, 2 (the blanket claims), 5, and 6's placeholders. The rest need a fact from you first.

## OWNER DECISIONS — pick the real model / confirm the fact
1. **Everyday insurance flow:** current says "a specialist calls and arranges it" (enquiry-led — matches the introducer-only model on record); ChatGPT says "self-serve handoff to Provenance's site." Pick the live flow; do not ship both.
2. **Charging basis & design fees:** current shows the real basis (per hour / per job) and design fees (£195–£2,950); ChatGPT blanks all to "price on enquiry". Spec §10 wants the real basis shown — confirm the numbers are current, or route to enquiry.
3. **Per-service credentials** actually held (NICEIC / MCS, pet-care GPS features, etc.).
4. **"One working day" reply SLA** — real?
5. **House Approved "reviewed annually"** — real?
6. **Sustainability metrics** — actually being measured?
7. **Pro application fee**, and **which My House surfaces** are live at launch.
8. **Delve Interiors** naming needs partner consent.
