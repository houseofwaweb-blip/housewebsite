08 / 09

Questions, asked and answered

Before you begin.

Can I cancel HoWA+ anytime?

Yes. Your home record stays with you, on the free tier. No data is held hostage.

How long does setup take?

Roughly two minutes for the Companion intake. The record builds itself from there.

What if my home doesn't fit a category?

HoWA learns one home at a time. Type, period, layout, garden — all configurable.

Where does my data live?

UK servers, encrypted at rest, never sold. Export at any time.

Do I have to use the booked services?

No. The marketplace is optional. You can use HoWA purely as a record.

What happens if I move?

The record transfers to the new home, or stays with the property — your call at the time.
